Browser workbench for encode / decode / hash / regex. Use it instead of stacking `echo | base64 | xxd`.

https://gchq.github.io/CyberChef/

Common recipes: From Base64, From Hex, URL Decode, ROT13, Magic (auto), To Hash (MD5/NTLM). Drag operations left → right; output is the next input.

Offline: Kali often has a local copy, or clone [gchq/CyberChef](https://github.com/gchq/CyberChef) and open `build/prod/index.html`.
